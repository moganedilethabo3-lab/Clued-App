<h1>Meaning behind "Clued"</h1> <img width="100" height="100" alt="clued_img6" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/09aafe6b-fbd4-4688-a357-071cd5008ccf" />


People usually fall victim to factual information disguised as Life hacks but are in fact Myths. And thats where Clued comes in. A quiz game that helps users from being clueless to being CLUED IN!  


<h1> Problem statement:</h1>

  Individuals are constantly exposed to lage volumes of Life hacks and advice through social media and online platforms. But given that such information is left unverified it could misleading for people.Hence making it difficult for people to distinguish whether what they are being told is a Life hacks or a Myths.

 <h1> Solution </h1>
Clued provides a quiz-based interface for users to engage with that helps them enhance their ability to identify the difference between myths and life hacks. Users are given real-life scenarios and immediate feedback supported by explanaions after completing the quiz.
